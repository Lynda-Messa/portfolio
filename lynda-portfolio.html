About the Artist

Colour, Memory
& the Walls of Blida

I'm Lynda Messaoudene — a self-taught visual artist from Blida, Algeria. My practice began in 2018 and has grown to encompass oil and acrylic painting, digital illustration, charcoal drawing, and large-scale mural frescoes.

I specialise in realism and semi-realism, with a deep focus on portraiture and landscape — subjects that carry the weight of human experience and collective memory.

My work has been featured in radio broadcasts, newspapers, and cultural publications across Algeria. In 2019 I painted portraits of historical figures of Blida as public murals as part of the Nerssem Bladi project.

Since 2024 I have run drawing and painting workshops for children. I have been an active member of L'Œil Artistique since 2022.

Public Art · Nerssem Bladi 2019

The Walls
To Remember

In 2019, I worked alongside other artists on the Nerssem Bladi project, painting large-scale portraits of historical figures from Blida. Together, we transformed public walls into a shared space for memory, identity, and remembrance.

🏛️
Historical Portraits
I painted figures who shaped Blida's identity and history, keeping their memory visible on its walls.
🗺️
Public Memory

Community Identity
